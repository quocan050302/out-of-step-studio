1|Twelve cowboys nearly bounced a river bridge into the water, just by walking in perfect time.
2|Texas, eighteen eighty-three. Eli, their leader, was proud of his rhythm.
3|He crossed first. Tap, tap, tap.
4|Behind him, every boot landed together.
5|The bridge began to bounce, gently, then like a clothesline in a storm.
6|A chain sang. A plank jumped. Eli's hat sailed into the river.
7|Luckily, the cowboys stayed dry. Unluckily, the fish kept the hat.
8|A steady beat feeds a bounce, like pushing a swing at the same moment, every time.
9|So cowboys learned to cross out of step. Eli still keeps perfect time, on dry land.
